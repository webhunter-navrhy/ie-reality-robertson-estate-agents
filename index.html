**Your Private Sanctuary Moments from the Heart of Donabate**

Discover the perfect blend of modern comfort and peaceful living in this immaculately presented 3-bedroom dormer bungalow. Nestled at the end of a quiet, tree-lined cul-de-sac, this 105-square-meter home offers a rare combination of privacy and convenience. Step inside to find a freshly updated interior, where sleek oak-laminated floors flow through bright, airy living spaces anchored by a stunning feature open fireplace.

The heart of the home is a gleaming, newly fitted kitchen designed for the modern chef, while the rest of the residence boasts thoughtful touches like a luxurious new upstairs bathroom and an expansive walk-in wardrobe.

Outside, your private, south-facing garden serves as a sun-drenched retreat for morning coffees or evening relaxation. With a garage, private driveway, and an integrated car charging point, every practical need is met with ease. Whether you’re looking to utilize the current oil-fired heating or transition to an optional gas connection, this home is move-in ready and waiting for you. Located just a pleasant 15-minute stroll from Donabate village and the train station, you’ll enjoy immediate access to local amenities while savoring the quiet charm of your own secluded haven. Don’t miss your chance to own a home that truly has it all.
